## About

Athalea builds the modeling, simulation and data infrastructure that pharmaceutical and academic research teams use to move from hypothesis to candidate faster. We are computational chemists, bioinformaticians, physicians and software engineers: we develop methods first, and turn them into software once they hold up to scientific scrutiny.

## What we do

- **Athalea Labs:** computational chemistry, bioinformatics analysis and drug discovery pipelines, from QSAR and molecular docking to molecular dynamics, binding free energies and ADME-Tox.
- **Athalea Research Suite:** one platform for the whole computational campaign.
- **Athalea Pocket:** clinical assessment apps built on published reference standards.
- **Athalea Open Source:** tools from our own research workflows, released openly for anyone to use and extend.
